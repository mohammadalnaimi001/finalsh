Our Philosophy

The Shakra Story

"A fragrance is not merely a scent — it is a memory waiting to be made, an identity waiting to be claimed, a story waiting to be told."

Founded 2018

Born in Amman,
Worn Worldwide

Shakra Perfume was born in the heart of Amman, Jordan — a city where ancient spice trade routes met modern ambition. Our founder, a perfume obsessive who spent years studying under master perfumers in Paris and Grasse, returned home with a vision: to create the world's finest niche fragrance house rooted in Middle Eastern heritage.

Today, Shakra Perfume ships to over 40 countries. Our fragrances have been worn at royal events, featured in international fashion editorials, and gifted between heads of state. Yet we remain a small, obsessive house — because size is the enemy of perfection.

Every Shakra fragrance takes between 18 months and 3 years to develop. We reject the first 97 iterations before arriving at the one that bears our name. We never rush, never compromise, never follow trends.
